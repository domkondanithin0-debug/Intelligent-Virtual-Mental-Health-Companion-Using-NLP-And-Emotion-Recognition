YOU DON'T HAVE TO FACE IT ALONE

Help & support 💗

Reaching out for help is a sign of courage.

🤝

Talk to someone you trust

Consider reaching out to a trusted friend, family member, teacher, or another supportive person. You deserve to be heard.

🩺

Professional support

A qualified mental health professional can help you understand your feelings and explore appropriate support.

♥

Immediate safety matters

If you may hurt yourself or someone else, or are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services now. Move near a trusted person and tell them directly that you need help. Do not stay alone if you feel unsafe.

India emergency number: 112

For mental health support in India, Tele-MANAS: 14416 (or 1-800-891-4416). If unavailable, contact emergency services or go to the nearest hospital.

Support service availability can change. In an emergency, use local emergency services.
